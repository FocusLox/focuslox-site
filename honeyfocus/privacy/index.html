HoneyFocus Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

HoneyFocus ("the app") is published by Cute and Honey LLC under the FocusLox brand ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

HoneyFocus does not have accounts, does not show ads, and does not track you. We do not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

Your focus sessions, tags, intentions, presets, honey jars and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We do not receive, see, or store this information on any server.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Delete everything, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Export backup, the app creates a file and you choose where to save it. If your phone's own backup is switched on (for example Google's device backup), your phone may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is controlled by you and your phone's maker, not by us.

Sharing your recap

If you tap Share on your weekly recap, the app hands a short text to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Notifications

The running-timer notification and the "session finished" alert are created locally on your device. No notification content is sent through our servers, and we do not use push notification tokens.

Purchases

HoneyFocus currently has no purchases. If a paid upgrade is offered in a future version, payment will be handled by Google Play or the App Store, and we will update this policy before it is released.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children

HoneyFocus is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
