HoneyFocus Support

Email support@focuslox.com with "HoneyFocus" in the subject. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I change the focus time?

On the Focus screen, tap the big time. Pick a quick choice, or type any length from 5 to 180 minutes.

When does a session fill a honeycomb cell?

A finished focus session of 10 minutes or more fills one cell. Seven cells make a jar of honey in your Pantry.

The timer ended late, or I got no alert

Check that notifications for HoneyFocus are allowed in your phone's settings. Then open Settings → Reliability check in the app and follow the steps for your phone. Some Android phones stop apps in the background unless you allow them in the battery settings.

How do I move to a new phone?

Use Settings → Export backup on the old phone, then Import backup on the new one.

Is my data backed up?

Your data stays on your phone. Export a backup from Settings whenever you like and keep the file somewhere safe.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete everything, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy.
