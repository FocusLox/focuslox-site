HoneyFocus

Focus sweetly. One session, one honeycomb cell.

HoneyFocus is a cute focus timer for studying and deep work. Each finished session fills one cell of your honeycomb, and seven cells make a jar of honey for your Pantry.

  • Pomodoro and stopwatch: short and long breaks, or open-ended deep work.
  • Any focus length: tap the time to pick a quick choice or type 5 to 180 minutes.
  • Honeycomb and Pantry: watch cells fill and collect jars of honey.
  • A timer you can trust: exact time even when your phone is locked or restarts.
  • Stats and streaks: today, this week and this month, with a gentle daily goal.
  • Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
