Quitsteady Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Quitsteady ("the app") is published by Cute and Honey LLC under the FocusLox brand ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Quitsteady does not have accounts, does not show ads, and does not track you. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

The habits you track, your streaks, check-ins, cravings, notes, and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. We do not receive, see, or store this information on any server.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Export a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you.

Notifications

Reminders and milestone alerts are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent through our servers, and we do not use push notification tokens.

Purchases

If you buy Quitsteady Premium, the payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm which purchases are active on your device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, and does not receive any of the habit data you enter. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children

Quitsteady is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
