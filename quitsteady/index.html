Quitsteady

Quit anything. One day at a time.

Quitsteady helps you quit a habit and stay quit, whether that's smoking, vaping, alcohol, sugar, caffeine, social media, gambling, or something only you know about.

  • Live streak: see exactly how long you've held out, down to the second.
  • Craving SOS: a breathing guide, your own reason for quitting, and a timer to ride out the urge.
  • Money and time back: watch your savings and reclaimed hours grow.
  • Milestones: from your first hour to five years, every one is celebrated.
  • Daily check-ins and a journal: notice what helps.
  • Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Quitsteady supports healthy habits. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional care.
